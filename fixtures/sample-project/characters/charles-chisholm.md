A scroll-wizard who makes documents the catalogue has never heard of, on the principle
that what was never accessioned can never be deaccessioned.

## The art

Charles works in scrolls because scrolls can exist outside the index.

[Deaccession](../world/deaccession.md) operates on entries. It finds a record in the
catalogue, strikes it, and the object follows the record down. The procedure is precise
and it is thorough, and it has one structural blind spot: it can only take what the
Archive has taken in first. A volume that was never accessioned has no entry to strike.
The committee can burn such a thing, which is honest and leaves a mark — but it cannot
make it never have been.

So Charles copies. He takes what matters out of the codices and onto the roll, unentered,
uncatalogued, unprotected, and therefore beyond the reach of quorum. His scrolls are the
only documents in the city that cannot be forgotten.

## What it costs him

Everything he makes is physically fragile in exact proportion to how permanent it is.

An accessioned book sits in a climate-kept stack behind civic locks, and if it burns, the
burn catalogue mourns it by name. A Chisholm scroll has none of that. No shelf, no lock, no
entry, and no mourning — if one is destroyed, there is no record it existed, because the
absence of a record was the entire point. He has spent his working life building a memory
that survives the committee and dies to a candle.

He is, as a consequence, a man who does not sit with his back to doors and who keeps
nothing important in one place.

## Why he is dangerous

The same blind spot cuts both ways. An unentered scroll cannot be struck from the
catalogue — and it also cannot be checked against it.

Charles can produce a document that no index will ever contradict, because no index
contains it. He is the only person in the city who can manufacture a permanent, unfalsifiable
truth, and he knows it. Whether he has ever done so is the question anyone who works with
him eventually has to ask, and he does not answer it in a way that settles anything.

## Against the others

He completes the triangle rather than duplicating either lead.

[Mara Vale](./mara-vale.md) can prove a thing was removed but never say what.
[Oren Pike](./oren-pike.md) can say what but never prove it. Charles can *make the record
stick* — he is the one who can turn what the two of them recover into something the city
cannot quietly take back.

Which means he is also the natural target. Mara and Oren are dismissable, and dismissable
people are safe. Charles is not dismissable, and there is exactly one procedure for a
problem the catalogue cannot solve.
